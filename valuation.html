Median is the middle constituent, which is what the quintile boundaries above are cut from. On this run they differ by more than a point, and neither is a substitute for the other.

Where the aggregate reads n/m — Russell 2000, trailing: index earnings are negative (−$9B); 825 of 1973 members lose money, so there is no aggregate multiple. This is a property of the index, not a missing number: an index whose members collectively lose money has no aggregate multiple, and the median and the profitable-only aggregate are the readable figures for it.
How this is computed, and why it will not exactly equal a vendor's number. Market value is FMP's full market cap, not float-adjusted, while S&P weights the index on float — so this is a full-cap aggregate and runs a little different by construction. Multiple share classes of one issuer are collapsed first (FMP reports Alphabet's entire 83.1T-scale cap under both GOOGL and GOOG); leaving them in doubles that issuer's weight and moved the trailing aggregate by 3.1% on this run. Loss-makers are kept in the earnings sum, which is the honest index number — dropping them always reads cheaper. The pack's Global-equity-valuations section reports a separate S&P 500 trailing P/E from MSCI/Damodaran; this one is computed here from constituents. They are two independent derivations of the same idea and land within a few percent — do not net them against each other, and do not treat a gap between them as a market signal. This one is 29.3x trailing.

The gap has a name. Against IVV's 25.1x, this page reads +16.4% including loss-makers and -3.1% excluding them. The second number is the whole story: published index multiples drop companies that lose money, which always reads cheaper. Our two figures bracket the vendor's rather than disagreeing with it.
Do not net these against each other and do not read a moving gap between them as a market signal. They are independent derivations, and a change in one can be a change of vintage or convention rather than of price. The pack applies the same rule to its own international rows, where crossing a trailing multiple with a forward average invented ~17 points of richness out of the definition alone.
